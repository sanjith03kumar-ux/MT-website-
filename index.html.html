Our Story

Da Weligama a Galle Road La Nostra Storia

What started with a passion for authentic Italian food in the beautiful coastal town of Weligama has grown into something even bigger. Mama Roma was created with one dream — to bring the true taste, warmth, and spirit of Italy to Sri Lanka. From our wood-fired pizzas and handmade Italian favourites to our warm and welcoming atmosphere, everything is inspired by the traditions we love. Our journey began in Weligama, where Mama Roma became a place for great food, good company, and the relaxed spirit of the coast.

Now, we proudly begin a new chapter with our Mama Roma Authentic Italian Restaurant & Sports Bar on Galle Road, Mount Lavinia. Bringing together authentic Italian cuisine, wood-fired pizzas, great drinks, live sports, music, and an energetic atmosphere, Mama Roma is a place for friends and families to come together, enjoy delicious food, and create unforgettable memories. Our promise remains the same: Authentic Italian taste. Fresh ingredients. Passion in every dish. From the shores of Weligama to the heart of Galle Road, Mama Roma continues to grow while staying true to its roots, passion, and love for Italian cuisine. Benvenuti a Mama Roma! Come for the pizza. Stay for the experience.
