Our Story

A Journey Through Italian Flavors

Nestled in the heart of Mount Lavinia, Mama Roma brings the soul of Italy to Sri Lanka. From hand-tossed pizzas to fresh pasta made daily, every dish is a tribute to authentic Italian craftsmanship.

Our chefs bring decades of experience from Italian kitchens, using imported ingredients and time-honored recipes passed down through generations.
